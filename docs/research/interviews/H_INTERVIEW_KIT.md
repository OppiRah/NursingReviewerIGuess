# Interview kit — H (nursing student reviewer)

## Rules

- Ask about what they **did last time**, not what they would do. Past behavior > opinions.
- Don't pitch the app until the very end (optional). Don't lead ("wouldn't it be nice if…").
- 15–20 min, casual, Taglish is fine. Ask permission to take notes; no recordings, no names in notes.
- Talk to 3–5 friends with different styles, including at least one "hardcore reader".

## Questions

1. Walk me through how you reviewed for your last exam, from getting the files to exam day.
2. Where did the files come from (instructor, blockmates, review center)? What format?
3. When you use ChatGPT/Claude for a reviewer, what exactly do you ask it? Can you show me one? How long does it take?
4. Have you ever found a wrong fact in an AI-made reviewer? What happened?
5. Once you have the reviewer, what do you do with it: read, highlight, quiz yourself? How?
6. Have you ever asked ChatGPT/Claude to quiz you? How did that go? Did you keep doing it?
7. Phone or laptop? When do you review (commute, before sleep, vacant time)?
8. Do you share reviewers with blockmates? How (GC, Drive, Messenger)?
9. What was the most frustrating part of reviewing last time?
10. Ever tried Quizlet, Knowt, NotebookLM or a reviewer app? Why did you stop, or never try?
11. Have you paid for anything study-related (review center, app, printing)? Roughly how much?

- Optional, last: describe the idea in one sentence and ask "What would make you actually open this before an exam?" (Treat the answer as weak evidence.)

## Kill criteria (decided before interviews)

H weakens or stops if most interviewees:

- say reading the AI reviewer is enough and don't want to be quizzed; or
- already quiz themselves via chatbot and are satisfied; or
- rarely study on their phone and don't share reviewers; or
- spend no extra time or effort on any workaround.

Signals that support H: copy-pasting between the chatbot and other tools; rewriting or printing reviewers; losing track of what they've mastered; distrust of AI errors; reviewers passed around the block.

## Note template (one file per conversation: research/interviews/notes/N1.md …)

- ID / date / year level / exam type (school or PNLE)
- What they did (behaviors, in order)
- Direct quotes (verbatim, anonymized)
- Tools used and time spent
- Interpretation (kept separate from the quotes)
- Supports / contradicts: H-H1 / H-H2 / H-H3
